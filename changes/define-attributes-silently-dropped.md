A `<define>` tag's own attributes are no longer dropped. `<define/D a=1 b="s"/>` compiled, and `${D.a}` read `undefined`; Marko makes the attributes properties of the define's value, and MX now does too. On html, preact, react and hono they are own properties of the function a call runs (a spread keeps its place, a repeated name is last-wins, and attributes named `name`, `length`, `caller` or `arguments` work), evaluated where the `<define>` is written and outside its params. A `<let>` written in one (`<define/D a=(count++)/>`) is now refused like in any attribute. A `content` attribute next to a body is dropped, as in Marko (the body is `content`), with the warning `MX_DEFINE_CONTENT_DROPPED` at the attribute; with no body it is an ordinary property. Solid and Angular cannot expose a property on their define (a hoisted function, a template reference) and now refuse a `<define>` with attributes with a positioned error instead of dropping them (a divergence from Marko, listed in `divergences.md`); Astro already refuses `<define>`.
A `Define` IR node now carries its `attrs`, so `IR_VERSION` goes from 3 to 4.
